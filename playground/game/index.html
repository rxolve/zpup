One scene, eight directions

Everything here was drawn by an AI model with Zpup: it wrote each object once, as a small 3D scene in JSON, and Zpup drew that scene from eight sides. The eight pictures cannot disagree, because they are one object. The dog also walks: its four poses are changes to that one scene, a few leg angles each, drawn from the same eight sides.

Move: arrow keys or WASD, or click where the dog should go. Turn a prop: click it.

The sheets

Each is one PNG with a frame list beside it, as render_sprites writes them. The same two files load in Phaser and PixiJS as they are. The scene is the JSON the model wrote.

What this is not

The dog turns, it does not walk: there are no animation frames, only directions. Zpup is for small props and things that turn, in one flat style.
